PDFPouch – Privacy & Support

DeutschPrivacy

Private PDF tools

PDFPouch processes user-selected PDFs locally on the device. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or PDFPouch cloud service.

Files and camera

Files are selected and saved through Android's system picker. Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document. Temporary files remain local.
